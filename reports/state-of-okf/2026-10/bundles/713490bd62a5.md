## What to change

### Files with no frontmatter block (3)

OKF v0.2 §11 asks every `.md` file other than `index.md` and `log.md` to start with a YAML frontmatter block holding a non-empty `type`. The smallest change is:

```yaml
---
type: Note
---
```
